Research Overview

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being applied to medical diagnosis through machine learning, deep learning and computer vision techniques. Researchers are using medical images, clinical records and multimodal data to support faster and more accurate diagnostic decisions.

Current research focuses on improving model accuracy, interpretability, clinical validation, data privacy and generalization across different patient populations.
