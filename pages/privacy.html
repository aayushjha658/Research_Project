Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Research AI may collect, how it may be used, and how users can manage their information.

Last updated: August 2026
01

Information We May Collect

Account Information

When you create a Research AI account, the platform may collect information such as your name, email address, and authentication-related information required to maintain your account.

Research Information

Depending on the features you use, Research AI may process research topics, searches, saved papers, citations, datasets, notes, literature-review information, and other research-related content submitted by you.

Technical Information

The application may process basic technical and security information such as IP address, browser information, session information, and security events for operation and security purposes.

02

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and protecting accounts.
  • Providing research and AI-assisted features.
  • Saving research activity and preferences where supported.
  • Detecting and preventing security abuse.
  • Improving platform reliability and functionality.
  • Communicating important account or service information.
03

Password & Authentication Security

Account passwords are intended to be stored using secure one-way password hashing rather than plain-text passwords.

Authentication sessions and security controls are designed to help reduce unauthorized access to user accounts.

Users should choose strong, unique passwords and should never share their account credentials.
04

Cookies & Sessions

Research AI may use session cookies and related technologies to maintain authenticated sessions, protect forms, and support security features.

These mechanisms help the application recognize an authenticated user during a session.

05

Third-Party Services & Sources

Some Research AI features may connect to or display information from third-party services, websites, repositories, APIs, or academic sources.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies when using features that interact with external services.

06

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security Notice

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own accounts and credentials.

07

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, or support legitimate operational requirements.

08

Your Choices

Depending on the available platform functionality, users may be able to update account information, manage saved research data, or request assistance with account-related privacy questions.

09

Children's Privacy

Research AI is intended for general research and educational use. If applicable law requires a different minimum age or additional consent requirements, those requirements will apply.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform, features, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with the applicable update date.

11

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Research AI support team.
