PAPER OVERVIEW

Abstract

Artificial intelligence has rapidly transformed modern healthcare by enabling automated, data-driven clinical decision support. This study investigates deep learning approaches for automated medical diagnosis using large-scale medical imaging and clinical datasets.

The research evaluates convolutional neural networks, vision transformers and hybrid architectures across multiple diagnostic tasks. Experimental results demonstrate that carefully optimized deep learning models can improve diagnostic accuracy while reducing the time required for manual analysis.

The study further examines model interpretability, generalization and computational efficiency. The findings highlight the importance of explainable AI techniques for building reliable and clinically useful automated diagnostic systems.
