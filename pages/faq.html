Research AI is an AI-powered research workspace designed to help users explore research topics, discover papers, review literature, analyze research and organize research-related resources.

Research AI is designed for students, researchers, academic professionals and anyone who wants a structured workspace for exploring and organizing research.

Open the Research Papers section and explore papers based on your selected research domain or topic. You can then open individual paper details for further exploration.

The Research Gap tool is designed to help you examine existing research and identify potential areas where further investigation may be useful.

The Literature Review workspace helps organize research literature and provides a structured place to review information related to your selected research topic.

The AI Assistant is intended to support research-related conversations, help explore concepts and assist with questions during your research workflow.
